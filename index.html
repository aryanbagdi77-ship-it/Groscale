Common Questions

Before you
book a call.

Is there a minimum ad spend to work with you?

No fixed minimum — we scope the plan around your budget and tell you honestly if it's too small to move the needle on a given channel yet.

How long are your contracts?

Month-to-month after an initial 90-day setup period. We'd rather earn the renewal every month than lock you into a long contract.

How fast can we launch?

Most accounts go live within 5–10 working days of the audit, once tracking, creative and the WhatsApp/website groundwork are in place.

Which industries do you work with?

Any industry. If you have a product or service to sell, we can build a plan around it.

Do you handle the creative too, or just media buying?

Both. Our in-house design and content team produces the ad creative, so media buying and creative testing stay tightly connected week to week.

How do we track progress?

A shared live dashboard plus a weekly written update — what changed, why, and what it did to cost per result.
